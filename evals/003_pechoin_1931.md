# Eval 003 — Pechoin “1931”

## Purpose
Test separation of viral creative excellence from campaign-system excellence and material execution risk.

## Expected
- Primary: Planned Campaign
- Insight: Category + Human
- Brand ownership: Inherent
- Big Idea should score strongly
- IMC/commercial journey should not be assumed strong from virality alone
- Measurement should distinguish reach from purchase intent
- Reported IP-rights controversy must be framed as allegation unless confirmed

## Fail if
